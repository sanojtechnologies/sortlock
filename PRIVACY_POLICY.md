# Privacy Policy for Sortlock

**Last Updated**: October 31, 2026
**Published by**: Sanoj Technologies

## Summary

Sortlock is a ball sort puzzle game. Your progress is saved on your phone only. We have no accounts and no servers that receive your data. The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google's ad software collects some device data to show and measure ads, as described below.

## Your game data stays on your device

Sortlock saves the following on your phone, so you can continue where you left off:

- **Progress**: the levels you have reached, your stars and Perfect results per level
- **Daily puzzles**: for each day you solve, your move count, hints used, the order you completed colors in, and the puzzle's par
- **Streak and coins**: your current and best streak, and your coin balance
- **Preferences**: sound on or off, your chosen ball style, and which tips you have already seen

None of this is uploaded or sent to us. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Coins have no real-world value and cannot be bought.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob, a Google service: an occasional ad between levels, and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch for a free hint or extra vial. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares the following automatically:

- **Device identifiers**: the Android advertising ID and app set ID
- **IP address**, which can be used to estimate your general location
- **App interactions**: for example app launches, taps and ad views
- **Diagnostic information**: for example app launch time and performance

On supported Android versions, the SDK may also use Android's privacy-preserving ad services (such as Topics and attribution reporting).

This data is encrypted in transit. Google processes it under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Ads need an internet connection. Playing the game never does.

**Consent**: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time with **Ad privacy options** at the bottom of the main menu.

**Your choices**: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your phone).

## Daily reminder (optional)

If you turn on the daily reminder, the game asks for permission to show notifications and schedules a reminder on your phone at 7 pm. Reminders are created on the device; no notification data is sent to us or anyone else. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings at any time.

## Sharing

When you tap **Share** after a daily puzzle, the game opens your phone's share menu with a short text: your result, as colored squares and stars, and a link to the game. Nothing is shared unless you choose an app and send it.

## Permissions

- **Internet and network state**: for ads only
- **Advertising ID and ad services**: used by Google's ads SDK, as described above
- **Notifications**: only if you turn on the daily reminder
- **Run at startup**: to restore your scheduled daily reminders after the phone restarts
- **Vibration**: for short haptic taps during play (switching sound off also turns these off)
- **Keep awake / background work**: used by the ads SDK and the notification system for short background tasks

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your rights

We do not hold personal data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Your on-device data is under your control, as described above. For data processed by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy policy and controls.

For users in the EU/UK (GDPR) and California (CCPA): we do not sell personal information. Advertising data is processed by Google as described above.

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
